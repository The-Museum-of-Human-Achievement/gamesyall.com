# Club Night 2026

September 12th, 2026 - 7-10pm

[1908 Kramer Ln, Suite 250 Austin, TX 78758](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FHAH5KfxzNsrBq7h6){:target="_blank"}

Welcome to Club Night! For the first time in Austin’s history we will have over a dozen Austin Area Game Developer Organizations ("Clubs") in the same room at the same time showing off games from their communities. We're talking Games Y'all, IGDA Austin, ATX Game Makers, Austin Game Developer Beer Night, North Austin Game Night, Austin Game Audio Meetup, Game Worlds, Austin Glitch State, Beyond the Build, Unity User Group, Austin Industry Unreal Engine Community, Game Developer Club(ACC), Game Developers Society at Texas State, EGaDS!, and the Hilltop Game Developer Club.

Club Night is a gamified way to learn about the 15+ Clubs in town, meet new friends, play new games, earn cool prizes, and be a part of the Austin game dev scene!

Companion App here: [https://thecoby.itch.io/club-night-2026](https://thecoby.itch.io/club-night-2026){:target="_blank"}

Free admission, donations welcome! RSVPs encouraged, or check-in at the door.

Swag Details

Many studios around town have generously donated quest reward swag. Donors will be announced at the event and updated on the Club Night page. Do you or your studio have Swag you would like to donate? Bring it to the event!

Venue + Health Details

This event will be indoors. We strongly encourage that everyone be vaccinated or have recently tested negative for COVID. Do not attend if you feel ill or were recently exposed to COVID. Hand sanitizer and masks will be available. Masks are welcome!

Games Y’all is a meetup fiscally sponsored by the Museum of Human Achievement, held every month at venues around Austin for indie devs, digital artists, and games fans. Games Y’all celebrates, amplifies, and supports the unique and underrepresented within indie games and beyond. Come play games, share what you’re working on, hang out and meet friends.
